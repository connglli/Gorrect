## Lifetime and qualification

Arm cleanup before acquire. After returned calls, ensure balanced release through `finally` on success, miss, worker failure, interruption and startup failure. Failure cleanup cancels rendezvous, serializes GC unpark/release while the owner is free, then interrupts/joins workers and unwinds resources; never join workers before releasing held control. Persist original and cleanup failures. WhiteBox waits have no Java timeout, and its native contract forbids concurrent release/request operations. A watchdog must not race release against blocked `runTo`. Host supervision bounds startup and each lease by the remaining process deadline; if the owner cannot return, terminate the target and report timeout without claiming release. Bound plan visits, transition events and mutation brackets through existing execution/evidence ceilings. Startup qualification precedes workload construction and supplies no workload certificate.

Require a matching boot-loaded WhiteBox client, diagnostic/WhiteBox flags, G1 selection, support query, exercised acquire/stops/idle/release, and usable independent recording on each admitted installation. Bounded release-17 diagnostic probes passed those calls on installed HotSpot 17 and 21 and proved a parked mutation lay outside the actual marking interval. Reused clients emitted unrelated registration warnings; this establishes exercised-method compatibility only. Source inspection corroborates HotSpot 21's installed build; HotSpot 17 source equivalence remains unverified. No production integration or active-mutation overlap is established.

Acceptance needs independent lease/phase fixtures, actual contained mutations, repeated visits, worker failure/cancellation and hung-call deadlines, wrong-cycle/partial/aborted evidence, offline verification/replay and legacy regression checks. WhiteBox, JNI transitions, recording and observation perturb timing/materialization; no placement, mandatory clearing, queue delivery or reclamation follows. Owning specifications change only during implementation.
